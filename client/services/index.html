FAQ

How does delivery timing work?

Answers on our process, timelines, and what to expect before you book.

Email us your question
01What's the typical timeline for a SaaS motion graphics project?

Most SaaS motion projects take 2–4 weeks depending on length, complexity, and feedback timing.

02How long does a 3D Industrial animation take?

A typical industrial animation takes 3–6 weeks once source files and the approved storyboard are ready.

03How long does a 3D offshore animation take?

Offshore projects usually take 4–8 weeks, depending on model complexity and technical review.

04Can we speed things up for a launch deadline?

Yes. Share your deadline at the start and we’ll recommend the fastest realistic production plan.

05How do we hand over files and assets at the end?

Final masters and agreed source files are delivered securely in all approved formats.

06What do you need from us before production starts?

A clear brief, brand assets, reference material, CAD or product files, and one point of contact.

07Where do revisions happen in the production process?

Reviews happen at agreed checkpoints: script, storyboard, style frames, animation, and final polish.
