01What information should I include in my initial brief?

Share your goals, timeline, and any references that capture the style you want. The more specifics you include, the more accurate the quote. We'll refine the rest during our first call.

02How do you structure pricing and payment?

Every project is quoted around scope, complexity, timeline, and deliverables. Payment milestones are agreed before production begins.

03What is the typical turnaround time?

Most projects take between two and eight weeks depending on scope. We’ll confirm a clear schedule in your proposal.

04Can I see drafts and give feedback?

Yes. You’ll review work at agreed checkpoints and give feedback before we move into the next production stage.

05Who owns the final files and source materials?

You receive the agreed final deliverables and source materials defined in your project proposal.
